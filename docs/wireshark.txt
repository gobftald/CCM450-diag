My expirence was that for the very simple test scenario when I sent fro my Mac a single 0 byte via UDP to CCM AP
then CCM sent an reset (ATZ\r) message via UART to ELM327, then after around 800ms response time CCM got the answer
from ELM327 (ATZ\r\r\ELM327 v1.5\r\r>) and sent it back to my Mac the overall response times measured on my mac were
quite a large variance. Above the 800ms there were a mimimum of 50 ms but sometime it increased up to several seconds.
But the average "oevrall long response times" were 950 - 1200 ms (800ms included).
